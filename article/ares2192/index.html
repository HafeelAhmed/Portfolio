ARES 2192

ARES 2192 is a sci-fi racing game. Between races you live on a space station, and the races themselves happen on a terraformed Mars. I've been on the team since an early stage of the demo, working mainly on AI and navigation.

Getting around the space station

When I joined, the space station was hard to navigate. Players regularly got lost and couldn't tell where they were supposed to go next.

I looked at where players were getting stuck and turned around, then worked through those spots one at a time. That meant fixing AI pathing, changing the level flow and making the environment easier to read, so players can see which way to go.

Working on a game that is still changing

The design is still moving, so I try to build systems that can be extended later without a rewrite. My changes go into the demo and are tested internally, and the feedback goes into the next pass.
